**PaletteLab** (`palettelab.in`) — free color discovery platform + harmonic generator for designers/devs/creators.

- **2 detail routes**: `?tab=palette-detail&palette=slug` and `?tab=color-detail&hex=...`
- Stack: Vite 6 + React 19 + TypeScript + Tailwind 4 + Express SSR-lite (`server.ts:1`) + `tsx`
