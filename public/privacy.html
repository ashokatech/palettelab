Privacy Policy — PaletteLab

Last updated: August 27, 2026 — Controller: PaletteLab (individual). Contact: hello@palettelab.co

1. What we collect

  • Local storage only: Liked/saved palettes and custom palettes are stored in your browser's localStorage. We do not have a server database for this yet.
  • Analytics (after you configure): Google Analytics 4 (if you add a GA4 ID) collects anonymized page views, device type, and referrer to improve the product.
  • Ads (after you configure): Google AdSense may set cookies to serve relevant ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.

2. No scraping, no resale

All palettes on PaletteLab are either hand-curated originals or mathematically generated via HSL/golden-ratio algorithms. We do not scrape or resell third-party palette collections. User-created palettes remain the user's intellectual property.

3. Cookies

We use only essential cookies for site function. If you enable GA4/AdSense, Google may set additional cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

4. Your rights

Clear localStorage to delete saved data. Contact us to request removal of any user-submitted palette.

5. Changes

We will update this page and bump the date above when we change data handling.
